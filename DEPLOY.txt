OFFGRID — Netlify site
======================
1. Drag this whole folder onto https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Online play (Versus / TAG online / global leaderboards) needs the OFFGRID game server
   running somewhere (see README.md in the source zip). Then put its wss:// address in
   offgrid-config.js in this folder and re-upload.
Everything else works without it.
